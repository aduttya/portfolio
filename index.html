I researched how AI decides who to recommend

The question

Buyers are starting to ask AI instead of Google. So I checked whether the two send them to the same companies.

What I found

They almost never do. On 19 of 27 software searches, AI and Google shared nothing. And AI often gave a different answer to the same question each time it was asked.

What I did

Built a new way to measure visibility, since a ranking means little when the answer keeps changing, and wrote it up as a research paper.
