About

Engineer turned product and growth lead, building for the AI search era.

I started as a blockchain developer. At Layers I designed a protocol that gives freelancers their own identity and data, wrote and deployed the smart contracts on Polygon and Ethereum, and led a team of developers, designers and marketers.

At Flozi, an AI growth engine for websites, I joined as a partner with two other founders. I brought the idea, defined the product, prototyped it, ran QA and releases, and led the growth team. That's where SEO and AEO became my specialty.

Today I lead SEO and AEO growth at Neue World and build Neue Analyse, an industry research and website audit product, end to end. I publish research on how AI systems decide who to recommend, and I'm moving toward research on human and AI interaction for AI safety.
